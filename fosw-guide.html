FOSW Purchase Approval System

A step-by-step user guide and reference document for FOSW board officers and review administrators.

Introduction

The Friends of Skinner West (FOSW) Purchase and Expense Approval automation workflow is a bespoke tool developed to digitize and speed up school purchase requests. It coordinates teacher requests, board reviewer sign-offs, and treasurer accounting inside a single portal.

By automating routing and notification schedules, the system guarantees compliance with FOSW accounting policies, tracks item budgets, maintains historical records, and frees board reviewers from long email chains.

⚡ The Element71 Solution

We designed this portal to run entirely on top of FOSW's Google Workspace, ensuring 100% data ownership, zero monthly licensing fees, and direct integration with your Google Sheets.

Connecting Your Google Account

Because the application integrates with Google Drive and FOSW's Sheets, users must authenticate using their Gmail account to gain access. Follow these steps to secure access:

1

Google Sheet Invitation

An administrator must first invite your email address as a Viewer or Editor to the FOSW Expense Approval master spreadsheet database. If you do not have permissions to the backing spreadsheet, you will be rejected at the portal login.

2

First-time Authentication

When visiting the portal web address, Google will prompt you to choose your account and grant permission for the "FOSW Expense & Purchase Approval" application.

3

Bypassing the Google Verification Screen

Because this is a private, custom-coded school app built for FOSW's internal workspace (rather than a public consumer app), Google will show a screen saying "Google has not verified this app".

⚠️ How to Proceed past the Warning Screen:

On the warning screen, click the small "Advanced" link at the bottom-left, then click the link that says "Go to FOSW Expense Approval (unsafe)". On the next screen, click "Allow". This is standard security behavior for internal Apps Script scripts and only needs to be performed once.

System Pages & Navigation

Once authorized, you will see a sleek dark-themed sidebar menu. Below is the purpose of each page:

  • All Active: The primary read-only dashboard table. Lists all active teacher requests in progress, showing their ID, requester name, item name, cost, reviewer votes, and status. Reviewers can search this list and click headers to sort items.
  • Pending Approval: Your personal review inbox. It shows visual cards representing requests that are currently waiting for your specific vote. Once you cast a vote, the card instantly disappears from this view.
  • Split Votes: Displays active items currently locked in a 2-approval / 2-denial tie. If you are logged in as the President or Treasurer, you will see resolve buttons to break the tie in bulk.
  • Approved Archive: History log of all requests that successfully cleared the 3/4 voting threshold and are ready for treasurer disbursement.
  • Rejected Archive: History log of all requests denied during review.
  • Admin Settings: Accessible to the President, Treasurer, and Developer. Allows editing reviewer first names, last names, and authorized emails. Also features toggles to enable/mute notification emails for the whole system or specific reviewer roles.

Approval Consensus Rules

The portal enforces a pure **3/4 vote consensus model** across four active reviewer roles: President, Vice President, Treasurer, and Secretary.

✓

Approval Threshold (3 Yes Votes)

If any item receives at least 3 Approved votes, the portal automatically marks the item as Approved, moves it to the archive, and schedules it for treasurer payout.

✗

Denial Threshold (3 No Votes)

If any item receives at least 3 Denied votes, the portal automatically marks the item as Denied, archiving it immediately.

!

Split Votes (2 Yes / 2 No)

If votes land on a 2-approval and 2-denial tie, the item is marked as Split Vote. The President or Treasurer can view these items on the Split Votes page and force a resolution.

ℹ️ Notes on Active Pipeline Queue

If an item has 1 approval and 2 denials, or 0 approvals and 2 denials, it remains in the active queue as Awaiting Votes because the remaining reviewers could still cast approvals to force a split vote or approved resolution.

System Operations & Notifications

The system works silently in the background of your Google Sheet. It handles:

  • Inbound Ingestion: Form entries submitted by teachers (via Google Forms or Tally.so) are recorded on the sheet and automatically trigger reviewer notification alerts.
  • Review Emails: Notification emails are sent with dynamic Approve/Deny buttons. You can vote directly from the email without opening the web portal.
  • Notification Toggles: Inside Admin Settings, you can mute notifications dynamically (e.g. for testing) or turn off email alerts for specific individuals. Disabled roles will be bypassed during email dispatches.
  • Audit Notes: Reviewers can write comments/notes in the input fields when voting. These comments are recorded permanently in the Google Sheet for auditor review.
